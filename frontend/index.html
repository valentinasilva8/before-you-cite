How it works

What it checks. Notices in one dated Retraction Watch extract, then a live Crossref check when you name a DOI or a title. The extract is loaded when the process starts. A request does not download it.

Who it is for. Someone about to cite a paper, a journal, or a publisher, and the course grader who can open how the agents worked.

How to use it. Paste the full citation as the model wrote it: title, authors, and link, not the link alone. A bibliography can hold up to 25 references. A journal or publisher name has to appear as it does in the extract. A topic alone gets a question back. A title with no DOI is compared with Crossref. At most three candidates are shown, and only when half their words are in the pasted title. No record is not a clean bill of health. It is a limit of this file.
