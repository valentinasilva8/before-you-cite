What it checks

Whether a paper you are about to cite really exists, and whether it has been retracted or corrected. We compare it against the Retraction Watch database (a dated copy, shown top right) and look it up live in Crossref, the registry most journals report to.

Who it is for

Students and researchers who got references from ChatGPT or another AI, and want to know which ones are safe to cite.

How to use it

Paste your references, up to 25. A title and authors is enough. If you have a DOI (the permanent ID most papers carry, like 10.1038/nature14539) or a link to the paper, include it, because that makes the check more reliable. Each reference gets a verdict. "No notice found" means we found no retraction, which is not proof the paper is sound.
