What it checks

Does it exist? A DOI is checked at Crossref, then doi.org, and the record is read from Crossref or DataCite. With no DOI, Crossref accepts only an exact title match, and a pasted first author and year must agree. If they agree, it counts as found. A difference means the link does not check out.

Was it retracted or corrected? The dated Retraction Watch file (top right) and Crossref's live updated-by and update-to notices, covering retractions, corrections, and expressions of concern.

How we read your paste. Fixed rules show the title, authors, year, and DOI so you can correct them before Run check. An AI model runs only when the rules cannot read a line, and it can only copy text you pasted. A line nobody can read is marked UNCONFIRMED, not skipped.

Who it is for

You got a reference from ChatGPT or another chatbot and you want to know if you can cite it.

How to use it

Paste what the chatbot wrote, including the link. Up to 25. A DOI is the paper's permanent ID. It looks like 10.1038/nature14539. A normal link works too.

  • DO NOT CITEA retraction is on record, or the link you pasted is the retraction notice.
  • DOESN'T CHECK OUTThe link goes to a different paper, or no registry has this DOI. It cannot be cited as written.
  • CHECK FIRSTA correction, expression of concern or other notice is on record. Read it before citing.
  • UNCONFIRMEDWe could not complete the check. Either we could not match it to a registry record (we search Crossref by title and accept only an exact match, so a slightly different title fails), or the live lookup did not return, or the paper exists in a registry that has no retraction data (such as arXiv), or it is not a paper. Check it yourself.
  • NO ISSUES FOUNDA registry has a record for the paper and we found no retraction or correction. We did not check whether the paper's content is relevant to your case, and this is not proof the paper is sound.
