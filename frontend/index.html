What it checks

Does it exist? Crossref, then doi.org. Record: Crossref or DataCite. Differing title, first author, or year is WRONG LINK. No DOI: exact title match; first author and year must agree. Else COULD NOT VERIFY, not proof it is fake.

Was it retracted or corrected? The dated Retraction Watch file (top right) and Crossref's live updated-by and update-to notices.

How we read your paste. Edit title, authors, year, and DOI before Run check. AI can only copy your paste, and only when the rules cannot read a line. Unreadable is COULD NOT VERIFY, not skipped.

Who it is for

You got a reference from ChatGPT or another chatbot and you want to know if you can cite it.

How to use it

Paste what the chatbot wrote, including the link. Up to 25. A DOI is the paper's permanent ID. It looks like 10.1038/nature14539. A normal link works too. "No notice found" means we found no retraction. That is not proof the paper is sound.

  • DO NOT CITEA retraction is on record for this citation.
  • CHECK FIRSTA notice other than a retraction is on record.
  • WRONG LINKThis link is a different paper from the pasted citation.
  • WRONG KIND OF LINKThis DOI is the retraction notice, not the original paper.
  • NOT FOUNDNo registration agency has this DOI.
  • COULD NOT VERIFYThe citation could not be read, or the live check did not return.
  • NO NOTICE FOUNDA registry has a record for the paper and we found no retraction or correction. That is not proof the paper is sound.
  • FOUND CAN'T CHECK RETRACTIONSA registry has a record for the paper, but that registry gives us no retraction data, so we could not check it there. Look it up on the publisher's page yourself.
  • NOT A PAPERThis item is not a paper citation.
