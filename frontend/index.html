What it checks

Does it exist? We look up the DOI at Crossref and DataCite. With no DOI, we search Crossref by title and also compare the title with the Retraction Watch file.

Retracted or corrected? We check the dated Retraction Watch file and Crossref's live notices for retractions, corrections and expressions of concern.

How we read your paste. Fixed rules read each line and show you the result before you run the check. When you run the check, an AI reader reads only lines with no DOI and no clear Author (Year). Title. shape, and it can only copy text you pasted. A citation line that cannot be read, such as a numbered line or one with a year in brackets, is marked UNCONFIRMED, not skipped.

What the results mean

  • DO NOT CITEA retraction is on record, or the link you pasted is the retraction notice.
  • DOESN'T CHECK OUTThe link goes to a different paper, or no registry has this DOI.
  • CHECK FIRSTA correction, expression of concern or other notice is on record. Read it before citing.
  • UNCONFIRMEDThe check did not finish. The row says why, for example a title that did not match one paper, a registry with no retraction data such as arXiv, or a line that is not a paper.
  • NO ISSUES FOUNDA registry has a record and we found no retraction or correction. This is not proof the paper is sound, and we did not check whether it is relevant to your case.
